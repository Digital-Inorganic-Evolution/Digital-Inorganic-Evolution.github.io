Digital Inorganic Evolution by VIEWLab 數碼無機進化賭博研究實驗室

By drawing inspiration from the evolution of organic life, we conduct an experiment in Digital Inorganic Evolution. The objective is to create a digital inorganic entity capable of surviving within the Horse Racing environment. Survival is defined as achieving a sustained positive net gain over the long term, interpreted simply as "winning", the energy rewarded must be larger than the energy consumed. The survival comparison assumes an energy consumption per hunting of HKD $10 each on Quinella bets
